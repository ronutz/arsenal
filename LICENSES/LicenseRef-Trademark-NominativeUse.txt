=========================

This is not a licence grant. It is a statement of the terms under which the
files it covers are held in this repository, written because the REUSE
specification requires every SPDX licence identifier used in a project to have
a corresponding text, and because "these are somebody else's trademarks and we
are not licensed to them" has no identifier in the SPDX list.

WHAT IT COVERS
--------------

Files marked `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Trademark-NominativeUse` are
third-party trademarks — vendor wordmarks and logos — reproduced in this
repository. They are held in public/img/marks/ and registered in
src/content/vendors/marks.ts.

none to give.

ON WHAT BASIS THEY ARE USED
---------------------------

Nominative use: a mark is used to identify the company that the surrounding
page is written about, and — the reason this set exists — to show the mark that
was that company's official one during the period the page covers, since this
site's subject matter spans four decades and a mark is itself dated evidence.

The site's author states that he verified each file against the respective
vendor's brand guidelines before it was included (2026-09-09).

CONSTRAINTS OBSERVED
--------------------

  * A mark identifies; it never decorates, and it never appears anywhere it
    could be read as the vendor endorsing this site or its author. Claims of
    authorisation are made in words, on the site's credentials page, and are
    verifiable there. A logo is not a credential.

  * Era matching: a mark is shown only for a period inside its own recorded
    range of official use.

  * No mark is recoloured, redrawn or restyled, and trademark symbols present
    in the supplied artwork are retained. Where a supplied file carried empty
    canvas around the artwork, the SVG viewBox has been trimmed to the
    artwork's bounding box so that marks of differing proportions render at
    comparable size; the clear space around each mark is then provided by
    layout. Geometry, colour and proportion are untouched.

IF YOU ARE A RIGHTS HOLDER
--------------------------

If you own one of these marks and object to its presence or to the manner of
its use, contact the site author and it will be removed. The registry is
designed so that removing a mark is a two-line change and the pages that
reference it degrade to plain text.

See also the TRADEMARKS section of the NOTICE file, the header of
src/content/vendors/marks.ts for the full rule set, and
scripts/check-marks.mjs for the part of it a machine can check.
