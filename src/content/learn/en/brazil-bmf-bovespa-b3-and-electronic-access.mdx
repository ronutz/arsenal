Brazil has one stock exchange, B3 (Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão), which the exchange's museum calls "the only stock exchange operating in Brazil" ([B3's museum on Google Arts & Culture, "Have you ever wondered what a stock exchange is?"](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/awVRzg4gVABXjg)), and its history explains most of what a broker, a trading firm or a technology vendor meets when connecting to it today. This part of the series opened by [Electronic trading: from the pit to the microsecond](/learn/electronic-trading-from-the-pit-to-the-microsecond) follows the São Paulo stock exchange, Bovespa, and the futures exchange BM&F, the Bolsa de Mercadorias e Futuros ([B3, 5 July 2024](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/como-era-a-bolsa-de-valores-em-1994.htm)), from their trading floors to B3, then reads B3's own rulebooks to show who may send orders, through which connections, most of them speaking the Financial Information eXchange (FIX) protocol, from where, and under whose supervision. After reading, you can trace the institutions and the platforms, read B3's access categories and its sponsored direct connection, explain what co-location offers, size the retail investor boom from B3's statistics, and say what the securities regulator, the central bank and the self-regulator each do. The floors themselves are the subject of [Open outcry: trading floors and the pregão](/learn/open-outcry-trading-floors-and-the-pregao), and the arrival of screens worldwide of [The screens arrive](/learn/the-screens-arrive-electronic-exchanges-from-nasdaq-to-globex).

## From the Bolsa Livre to B3

B3 S.A., Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão, dates its line to the Bolsa Livre, created in 1890 as Brazil's first stock exchange; the São Paulo exchange took the name Bolsa de Valores de São Paulo (Bovespa) in 1967, merged with BM&F in 2008 to form BM&FBovespa, and merged with Cetip in 2017 to form B3 ([B3 Bora Investir glossary, B3 S/A, modified 23 July 2025](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/glossario/b3-s-a)). The table sets out the milestones the exchange records in its 2024 reference form, its investor relations pages and its museum, each sourced in the paragraphs that follow.

### Two exchanges, one city

B3's 2024 reference form tells the São Paulo story most fully: the Bolsa Livre was an association of São Paulo brokers created in 1890 that did not survive the financial crisis of 1891; in 1895, with the state government's support, the Bolsa Oficial de Fundos Públicos de São Paulo was set up, renamed Bolsa Oficial do Estado de São Paulo in 1936 when it became an agency of the state finance department; from the 1960s Bovespa was a mutual, not-for-profit exchange, until its demutualization in 2007 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). B3's glossary calls the Bolsa Livre Brazil's first stock exchange, while B3's museum, MUB3 (Museu da Bolsa do Brasil) ([MUB3](https://www.mub3.org.br/)), describes it more narrowly as the first stock exchange in São Paulo, organized by brokers led by Emílio Rangel Pestana, and records a Rio de Janeiro exchange that operated for more than 150 years ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture, "Have you ever wondered what a stock exchange is?"](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/awVRzg4gVABXjg)). In 2000, aiming to concentrate all Brazilian share trading in one exchange, Bovespa led an integration programme with the eight other Brazilian stock exchanges ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)).

The futures exchange came later. B3's reference form says the Bolsa de Mercadorias e Futuros (BM&F) was founded in January 1986 and became Latin America's main derivatives centre in the 1990s ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). The museum adds that it was created by Bovespa's board of directors as the Bolsa Mercantil e de Futuros to trade derivatives such as futures on commodities, currencies and gold, that in 1991 it integrated the Bolsa de Mercadorias de São Paulo while keeping the acronym BM&F, and that in 1997 it merged operationally with the Brazilian Futures Exchange (BBF) ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture, Manoel Felix Cintra Neto](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/iwWxkfwSiAYGAQ)). The reference form instead describes the exchange as "Denominada inicialmente de Bolsa de Mercadorias de São Paulo", initially called the Bolsa de Mercadorias de São Paulo ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)); the two accounts of the early name do not match, and neither explains the other.

### From the floor to the screen

Automation began long before the screens. In the early 1970s Bovespa automated the recording of floor trades and began disseminating quotes electronically, and early in the 1990s it introduced, beside the floor, the Computer Assisted Trading System (CATS), developed by the Toronto exchange and replaced in the mid-1990s by a system bought from the Paris exchange ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). The floors were large. B3 says the open outcry system peaked in the 1990s, when Bovespa had about 1,400 floor traders and BM&F another 1,200 ([B3, 4 April 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/avanco-em-tecnologia.htm)). Electronic trading arrived alongside them: in an interview B3 published for the thirtieth anniversary of the real, André Demarco, a floor controller in 1994 and later a director of the self-regulator BSM, recalled that about ten companies' shares, 95% of trading, were traded on the floor while low-liquidity shares were already traded "por meio de sistemas eletrônicos chamados CATS e WINCATS", on the electronic systems CATS and WINCATS ([B3, 5 July 2024](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/como-era-a-bolsa-de-valores-em-1994.htm)). On the first day of the real, 1 July 1994, Bovespa recorded 4,204 trades worth R$74,974,160 ([B3, 5 July 2024](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/como-era-a-bolsa-de-valores-em-1994.htm)).

BM&F moved in steps. On 31 January 2000, its fourteenth anniversary, it joined the Globex alliance of exchanges, and in September 2000 it launched its electronic Global Trading System (GTS) in the derivatives market, where the electronic session reached 7% of transactions outside open outcry hours ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture, Manoel Felix Cintra Neto](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/iwWxkfwSiAYGAQ)). Bovespa's open outcry ended on 30 September 2005 ([B3 Bora Investir, 30 September 2025](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/fim-do-pregao-viva-voz-faz-20-anos-e-b3-celebra-historia-com-exposicao/)).

BM&F's floor lasted four more years. B3's reference form records that open outcry at BM&F ended on 30 June 2009, when all trading became exclusively electronic ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)), and the museum likewise says the advance of electronic trading "brought a definitive end to open outcry trading on June 30, 2009" ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture, "Market Voices"](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/TwUBZY0fl7VP-A)). A B3 news page of 2025 gives an earlier date, saying the system was extinguished "em meados de 2006, pela BM&F", in mid-2006 ([B3, 4 April 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/avanco-em-tecnologia.htm)), without explaining the difference.

### Demutualization, mergers and Cetip

Both exchanges demutualized in 2007. Bovespa's demutualization was approved on 28 August 2007, turning the holders of its seats into shareholders of Bovespa Holding, whose shares began trading in October; BM&F separated its members' ownership from their trading rights, agreed in September that General Atlantic would acquire 10% of its capital, and listed its shares on 30 November 2007 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)), in an offering that, according to the museum, attracted a record 250,000 investors ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture, Manoel Felix Cintra Neto](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/iwWxkfwSiAYGAQ)). On 8 May 2008 the two were integrated into a new company renamed BM&FBOVESPA S.A., which the CVM registered as a market operator on 19 May 2009 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). Cetip, created with Central Bank support by a vote of the Conselho Monetário Nacional in 1984, began operating in March 1986 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)); its boards and BM&FBovespa's approved a merger in 2016, the Central Bank, the Comissão de Valores Mobiliários (CVM) and the competition authority approved it in 2017, and B3 was created in March 2017 ([B3 investor relations, history, last updated 30 June 2020](https://ri.b3.com.br/en/?p=42)); the combination was consummated on 29 March 2017 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). The museum describes Cetip's purpose as registering, clearing and holding in custody private securities traded over the counter ([MUB3 on Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/awVRzg4gVABXjg)). The competition authority is the Conselho Administrativo de Defesa Econômica (Cade), a federal agency linked to the Ministry of Justice ([Cade, institutional page](https://www.gov.br/cade/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional)).

## PUMA, built with Chicago

The platform Brazil trades on today began as a partnership with the Chicago Mercantile Exchange's parent, CME Group. On 23 October 2007 CME Group and BM&F signed a non-binding letter of intent under which CME would take roughly 10% of BM&F and BM&F roughly 2% of CME, and each exchange's network would route orders to the other's products ([CME Group, 23 October 2007](https://www.cmegroup.com/media-room/press-releases/2007/10/23/cme_group_and_thebrazilianmercantilefuturesexchangeagreetocross-.html)). On 12 February 2010 the two became "Global Preferred Strategic Partners": they agreed to develop jointly a multi-asset electronic trading platform for BM&FBovespa's cash equities and derivatives, expected to launch in early 2011, and BM&FBovespa agreed to raise its stake in CME Group to 5%, "the same approximate stake CME Group currently has in BM&FBOVESPA" ([CME Group and BM&FBovespa, PR Newswire, 12 February 2010](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cme-group-and-bmfbovespa-to-become-global-preferred-strategic-partners-jointly-develop-a-new-multi-asset-class-trading-platform-and-to-expand-cross-equity-ownership-84214252.html)). BM&FBovespa's chief executive said the new standard would be "based on technology derived from the CME Globex trading system" and on new technology created by both parties ([CME Group and BM&FBovespa, PR Newswire, 12 February 2010](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cme-group-and-bmfbovespa-to-become-global-preferred-strategic-partners-jointly-develop-a-new-multi-asset-class-trading-platform-and-to-expand-cross-equity-ownership-84214252.html)).

B3's reference form records how the plan was carried out: definitive agreements for an initial term of 15 years were signed on 22 June 2010, BM&FBovespa raised its stake in CME Group from 1.8% to 5% in July 2010, and the PUMA Trading System entered operation in the BM&F segment in the second half of 2011, in the Bovespa segment in the first half of 2013 and in private fixed income in July 2014, with its infrastructure fully renewed in 2017 ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)). The partnership did not run its term: BM&FBovespa sold 20% of its CME shares in September 2015 and the rest under a material fact notice of 7 April 2016, to fund the Cetip combination ([B3, Formulário de Referência 2024, version 16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/5fd7b7d8-54a1-472d-8426-eb896ad8a3c4/8d8a1c1b-991e-904f-e699-a9cb7cc6d9e5?origin=1)), and B3's investor relations page dates the end of the partnership to 2016 ([B3 investor relations, history, last updated 30 June 2020](https://ri.b3.com.br/en/?p=42)). In its 2022 disclosure under the international principles for financial market infrastructures, B3 describes PUMA as the electronic system on which it trades equities, financial and commodity derivatives, fixed income, federal government bonds and spot foreign exchange ([B3, Princípios para Infraestruturas do Mercado Financeiro, March 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/55/F1/B4/04/04A11810C493CD08AC094EA8/B3%20PFMI%20DISCLOSURE%202022%20PT%20SEM%20MARCAS.pdf)).

### EntryPoint: B3's FIX dialect

Orders reach PUMA through EntryPoint, a unified interface for equities, fixed income, derivatives and foreign exchange that "is based on the 4.4 version of the Financial Information Exchange ("FIX") Protocol" ([B3, EntryPoint Messaging Guidelines 2.9.31, 16 December 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FF/84/5F/93/DB81D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/EntryPointMessagingGuidelines.pdf)). How FIX itself works is covered in [Anatomy of a FIX message and the session layer](/learn/anatomy-of-a-fix-message-and-the-session-layer); what matters here is how B3 fills it in. The same guidelines show a real fragment of a NewOrderSingle (35=D) carrying three parties:

`...35=D…453=3|448=123|447=D|452=7|448=TRDR|447=D|452=36|448=BVMF|447=D|452=54|…`

| Tag | Name | Value | Meaning in B3's specification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35 | MsgType | D | NewOrderSingle |
| 453 | NoPartyIDs | 3 | three parties follow |
| 448 | PartyID | 123 | firm code |
| 447 | PartyIDSource | D | identifier source D, as B3 specifies |
| 452 | PartyRole | 7 | Entering Firm |
| 448 | PartyID | TRDR | trader code |
| 447 | PartyIDSource | D | identifier source D |
| 452 | PartyRole | 36 | Entering Trader |
| 448 | PartyID | BVMF | Sender Location code for desk traders |
| 447 | PartyIDSource | D | identifier source D |
| 452 | PartyRole | 54 | Sender Location |

B3 uses the example to warn that its reply may list the same parties in a different order, so client systems must not rely on the order of repeating groups ([B3, EntryPoint Messaging Guidelines 2.9.31, 16 December 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FF/84/5F/93/DB81D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/EntryPointMessagingGuidelines.pdf)). In the FIX repository, PartyID (448) and PartyRole (452) date from FIX 4.3 ([FIX Latest, PartyID](https://orchimate.org/fixtrading/fix-latest/fields/PartyID); [FIX Latest, PartyRole](https://orchimate.org/fixtrading/fix-latest/fields/PartyRole)). The same guidelines list the order types by segment: a limit order (40=2) is accepted in all four segments, and a market order (40=1) carries a protection price calculated by the matching engine so that it cannot sweep the book without limit ([B3, EntryPoint Messaging Guidelines 2.9.31, 16 December 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FF/84/5F/93/DB81D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/EntryPointMessagingGuidelines.pdf)).

EntryPoint now comes in two encodings. B3's connectivity policy describes FIX order entry sessions based on FIX 4.4 and binary sessions in Simple Binary Encoding (SBE), plus market data sessions based on FIX 5.0 in FIX Adapted for STreaming (FAST) or SBE, noting that SBE "offers lower latency when compared to the conventional FIX protocol" ([B3, Commercial Policy for Connectivity Services](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/47/92/29/A6/EED1F910ADC36BE9AC094EA8/Commercial%20Politcy%20for%20Connectivity%20Services.pdf)). The binary gateway went live in derivatives on 4 December 2023 and in equities in three waves on 29 January, 19 February and 4 March 2024 ([B3, Circular Letter 015/2024-PRE, 24 January 2024](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/56/46/59/0D/3664D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/OC%20015-2024%20PRE%20%20Lan%C3%A7amento%20do%20Gateway%20Binario%20Segmento%20Bovespa%20%28Binary%20EntryPoint%29%20-%20EN.pdf)). B3 says the change, among others, cut its latency, the time between an order's arrival and its execution after validation, from 1.2 milliseconds in 2023 to about 350 microseconds, and its technology vice-president set a goal of below 300 microseconds by 2026 ([B3, 4 April 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/avanco-em-tecnologia.htm)). The encodings themselves are the subject of a later part on FIX encodings and successors.

## Who may trade: B3's participant categories

Only institutions holding an access authorization may trade on B3. Its access regulation lists the authorized participants as the participante de negociação pleno (PNP, full trading participant), the participante de negociação (PN, trading participant), the clearing member, the settlement participant, the custody agent, the registration participant and the market infrastructure; the PNP holds an authorization to trade, while the PN's authorization covers intermediating operations through a PNP ([B3, Regulamento de Acesso, version of 9 August 2021](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/54/77/25/84/C9A2B71027085EA7AC094EA8/Regulamento%20de%20Acesso%20da%20B3_20210809.pdf)). B3's access manual, in its version of 30 June 2025, sets the economic, operational and technical requirements for each category and adds the foreign participant and the external system ([B3, Manual de Acesso, 30 June 2025](https://sistemasweb.b3.com.br/normativos/MANUAL_DE_ACESSO_DA_B3-30062025.pdf)).

## How orders reach B3: connections and the sponsored direct connection

B3's trading procedures manual sorts connections by category, execution method, origin and hosting place, and gives each combination an alphanumeric identification ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)). The manual describes two kinds of connection:

- the **participant connection** (conexão de participante de negociação pleno), used by a PNP, a PN or a foreign participant to register offers in its own name or on behalf of clients, through a trading desk, an investment adviser, automatic position zeroing, automated direct offers or a supervisory session that may only cancel;
- the **sponsored direct connection** (conexão direta patrocinada), which "deve ser utilizada por comitente", must be used by a client, always under the responsibility of a PNP, PN or foreign participant, to register offers exclusively in its own name, for its proprietary book, directly in the trading system ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)).

A sponsored client receives market data in real time and may send, modify or cancel offers within the pre-trade limits its sponsor sets, and the sponsor may grant the connection only if its intermediation contract with the client contains the clauses B3 requires ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)). The connection's origin is identified by the user of the order or execution management system attached to it, and may be the participant, an independent technology provider, or, only for sponsored connections, the client itself; the equipment may sit at the participant, the client, a provider's data centre or B3's ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)). An independent provider must sign a specific agreement with B3, certify its solution, run with high availability, leave the participant at least one alternative way to trade in a contingency, and keep a disaster recovery plan ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)).

### Four direct market access models, and the names that replaced them

For years B3 described direct market access (DMA) as four models. Its English page for non-resident investors still lists them: "Traditional DMA (Sponsored by a broker)", "DMA via a DMA Provider", "DMA via Direct Connection" and "DMA via Colocation", adding that "For all the DMA models the investor must be registered to a B3 licensed Brokerage House" ([B3, Direct Market Access (DMA), undated](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/non-resident-investor/how-to-get-connected-bm-fbovespa/direct-market-access-dma.htm)). The EntryPoint guidelines keep the numbering in their tables of CompIDs and access codes, which replaced the old Bovespa numeric ranges ([B3, EntryPoint Messaging Guidelines 2.9.31, 16 December 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FF/84/5F/93/DB81D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/EntryPointMessagingGuidelines.pdf)):

| Category in the guidelines | SenderCompID format | Sender Location code | Former Bovespa range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desk traders | SFFFXXXX (B3 front end) or CFFFXXXX (vendor front end) | BVMF | 770-999 |
| DMA1 | CFFFXXXX | DMA1 | 300-309, 360-399, 400-499 (equities); 500-509, 560-599 (derivatives) |
| DMA2 | PFFFXXXX | provider code | 600-649 |
| DMA3 | YFFFXXXX | DMA3 | 650-655 |
| DMA4 | XFFFXXXX | COLO0 or COLO1 | 656-769 |

Read against the English page, DMA2 is the provider model, whose code is the provider's own, and DMA4 the co-location model, whose codes begin with COLO. FFF stands for the firm's mnemonic and XXXX for a suffix, and "DMA Providers (DMA2), sponsored by multiple Firms, must use different FIX sessions to submit orders from each Firm" ([B3, EntryPoint Messaging Guidelines 2.9.31, 16 December 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FF/84/5F/93/DB81D8103152D4C8AC094EA8/EntryPointMessagingGuidelines.pdf)). Commercially, B3 retired the numbers: its connectivity policy records that on 30 August 2021 the nomenclature changed "From DMA 1, 2, 3, and 4 to Sponsored Direct Connections or Direct Market Access (DMA) connections", and that every order entry session is now classified by who administers the execution solution, which agent has access to it, and where it is hosted ([B3, Commercial Policy for Connectivity Services](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/47/92/29/A6/EED1F910ADC36BE9AC094EA8/Commercial%20Politcy%20for%20Connectivity%20Services.pdf)). B3's current table of Sender Location codes follows that logic: DMACF identifies a sponsored direct connection originated by the client from another data centre, DMACC the same from B3's co-location, and DMAPF, DMAPC and the DMAXXXX forms the variants whose origin is the participant or a provider ([B3, Sender Location table, 2 January 2025](https://b3.com.br/data/files/C6/75/31/C9/CBC2491029BEEC39AC094EA8/Sender%20Location_02012025%20.pdf)). In FIX terms, the idea comes from SenderLocationID (tag 142), which identifies a message originator's location ([FIX Latest, SenderLocationID](https://orchimate.org/fixtrading/fix-latest/fields/SenderLocationID)).

### What the CVM requires

The securities regulator wrote DMA into its intermediation rule. Resolução CVM 35 of 26 May 2021 lists DMA systems among the ways a client may transmit an order (article 12), requires an intermediary that receives DMA orders to try to identify their origin and to keep a pre-trade risk control system with operational limits and error parameters (article 16), allows non-resident intermediaries and portfolio managers to use DMA terminals only if their home regulator has a cooperation agreement with the CVM or signs the multilateral memorandum of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) and they are registered at home (article 17), and requires each market operator to adopt DMA rules approved by the CVM, with supervision by the operator and its self-regulator (articles 18 and 19) ([CVM, Resolução CVM 35, 26 May 2021](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/cvm_institucional/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol035.pdf)). B3's tool for those limits is Line EntryPoint, which brokers use to set trading limits for clients on DMA 3 and DMA 4 ([B3, EntryPoint](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/solutions/platforms/puma-trading-system/for-developers-and-vendors/entrypoint-8AA8D0CC95C8AC5601961178692278BE)). In other markets the equivalent duty sits in rules on market access, the subject of a later part on pre-trade risk and market access.

## Co-location in Santana de Parnaíba

B3 runs its active data centre on Rua Ricardo Prudente de Aquino, in Tamboré, Santana de Parnaíba, in greater São Paulo, and its disaster recovery centre on Rua Quinze de Novembro in the old city centre ([B3, Manual de Acesso à Infraestrutura Tecnológica, version 8.5, 13 February 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/B9/F0/1F/4F/DC7CF91094DCD8F9AC094EA8/2026%20-%20Manual%20de%20Acesso%20a%20Infraestrutura%20Tecnologica%20da%20B3.pdf)). Its co-location policy says the Santana de Parnaíba centre was inaugurated in 2017 and holds a Tier III certificate from the Uptime Institute ([B3, Política Comercial de Serviços de Co-location, version 3.3, 20 January 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/3E/03/2D/39/558C38101E311E28AC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20Colocation_PT_2022%20v3%20Abril22.pdf)).

Co-location on B3's local area network (LAN), in B3's words, offers "o menor nível de latência de infraestrutura de rede", the lowest network latency for electronic trading, because customer equipment sits on the same local network as the trading gateways; each customer gets two pairs of multimode fibre at 10 Gbps to separate switches, with routed point-to-point links and BGP ([B3, Manual de Acesso à Infraestrutura Tecnológica, version 8.5, 13 February 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/B9/F0/1F/4F/DC7CF91094DCD8F9AC094EA8/2026%20-%20Manual%20de%20Acesso%20a%20Infraestrutura%20Tecnologica%20da%20B3.pdf)). To keep conditions equal, every cable inside the co-location area has the same length, and B3 quotes laboratory latencies of 22 microseconds to the TCP services, such as the order entry gateways, and 11 microseconds to the multicast market data, with about 30 and 15 microseconds more for customers reaching them through a cross connection to the edge of the Rede de Comunicação B3 (RCB), the exchange's own network ([B3, Política Comercial de Serviços de Co-location, version 3.3, 20 January 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/3E/03/2D/39/558C38101E311E28AC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20Colocation_PT_2022%20v3%20Abril22.pdf)).

The same policy lists who may rent racks: trading participants, market data distributors, order routing providers that sell order and execution management systems to other tenants by cross connection, managed service providers, connectivity providers, dedicated circuit providers and RCB carriers ([B3, Política Comercial de Serviços de Co-location, version 3.3, 20 January 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/3E/03/2D/39/558C38101E311E28AC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20Colocation_PT_2022%20v3%20Abril22.pdf)). Cross connections between tenants are limited to order routing, market data, drop copies and monitoring; those between a provider and its clients or participants are allowed with the parties' consent, while others, such as between clients of the same economic group or between a participant and a client, need B3's prior approval ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)). B3 says that high-frequency traders, its main co-location audience, account for about 40% of trades in the Brazilian equities market ([B3, 4 April 2025](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/avanco-em-tecnologia.htm)); that figure and the dispute around high-frequency trading are examined in [High-frequency trading: what it is and the dispute](/learn/high-frequency-trading-what-it-is-and-the-dispute), and co-location worldwide in [Colocation, microwaves and the race for latency](/learn/colocation-microwaves-and-the-race-for-latency).

## The participants: brokers, providers and networks

Brokers, banks and securities distributors connect through the exchange's own network, the RCB, and must use two different carriers on physically distinct routes ([B3, Manual de Acesso à Infraestrutura Tecnológica, version 8.5, 13 February 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/B9/F0/1F/4F/DC7CF91094DCD8F9AC094EA8/2026%20-%20Manual%20de%20Acesso%20a%20Infraestrutura%20Tecnologica%20da%20B3.pdf)); the networks around them are described in [FIX networks: hubs and managed connectivity](/learn/fix-networks-hubs-and-managed-connectivity). B3 also lets a broker run its desk on infrastructure from an independent provider, covering order generation, market data, trading screens, the order management system, risk limits, connectivity and drop copies, provided the provider signs an order routing authorization with B3 ([B3, Manual de Acesso à Infraestrutura Tecnológica, version 8.5, 13 February 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/B9/F0/1F/4F/DC7CF91094DCD8F9AC094EA8/2026%20-%20Manual%20de%20Acesso%20a%20Infraestrutura%20Tecnologica%20da%20B3.pdf)). Three examples show the range, each as its own pages or its shareholders describe it.

**CM Capital.** In December 2010 Compagnie Financière Tradition, the Swiss-listed interdealer broker (IDB), agreed to buy 30% of CM Capital Markets Holding from The Royal Bank of Scotland NV, describing the group as an IDB established in Madrid in 1986 and in São Paulo in 1998, specialized in interest rate and equity derivatives, and "one of the most active IDB on BM&F Bovespa" ([Compagnie Financière Tradition, 3 December 2010](https://direct.tradition.com/media/162784/press%20release%20tradition-cmh%20_%20uk.pdf)). CM Capital's own page for high-frequency clients, read in November 2026, repeats that timeline, calls the firm an independent broker that represents only one party in each negotiation, offers to guide foreign firms through B3's co-location and connectivity rules, and describes itself as "Brazil's largest independent investment brokerage", a claim it makes for itself ([CM Capital, HFT Solutions](https://mkt.cmcapital.com.br/hft)).

**Cedro Technologies.** Cedro says it was founded in 2005 as a start-up from a laboratory at the Universidade Federal de Uberlândia ([Cedro Technologies, about](https://cedrotech.com/sobre-nos/)). It sells an order management system with pre-trade risk and multi-asset routing that it says can connect to platforms such as Bloomberg and Sinacor ([Cedro Technologies, OMS Cedro](https://cedrotech.com/produtos/oms-cedro/)), offers order entry "via FIX", and says it supports "qualquer um dos modelos de Direct Market Acess (DMA) disponíveis na B3", any of B3's DMA models ([Cedro Technologies](https://www.cedrotech.com/)).

**RTM.** RTM, the Rede de Telecomunicações para o Mercado, founded in 1997 by the market association now called the Associação Brasileira das Entidades dos Mercados Financeiro e de Capitais (ANBIMA) and today a company of ANBIMA and B3, connects financial institutions across Brazil and is one of B3's four access modes ([RTM, institutional page](https://rtm.net.br/institucional/); [B3, Manual de Acesso à Infraestrutura Tecnológica, version 8.5, 13 February 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/B9/F0/1F/4F/DC7CF91094DCD8F9AC094EA8/2026%20-%20Manual%20de%20Acesso%20a%20Infraestrutura%20Tecnologica%20da%20B3.pdf)).

## The investor boom in B3's numbers

B3 counts retail investors in two ways, and the difference matters. An account is a relationship with one broker; a CPF, the Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas taxpayer number that is unique to each person ([Receita Federal, Meu CPF](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/meu-cpf)), counts the person once however many brokers they use. B3 began publishing CPF counts in 2021 because many investors had accounts at several brokers, calling the CPF "um termômetro mais preciso", a more precise thermometer ([B3, 3 November 2021](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/4-milhoes-de-pfs.htm)). The table separates the two.

| October 2021 | 4 million | 3.4 million | B3, 3 November 2021 |
| December 2021 and January 2022 | 5 million (January 2022) | 4.2 million (December 2021) | B3, 4 February 2022 |

B3 says more than 2 million people made their first stock market investment between April 2019 and April 2020, that accounts passed 3.2 million in October 2020, and that the number of women investors rose from 179,392 in 2018 to 809,533 in 2020 ([B3, 14 December 2020](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/investidores.htm)). In October 2021 it reached 4 million accounts and 3.4 million distinct CPFs ([B3, 3 November 2021](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/4-milhoes-de-pfs.htm)); in January 2022, 5 million accounts, with 4.2 million distinct CPFs at the end of 2021 and 13.1 million individuals in all once fixed income was included ([B3, 4 February 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/5-milhoes-de-contas-de-investidores.htm)). Growth then slowed: B3's quarterly book counted 5.3 million variable-income investors at the end of 2024, about 300,000 more in the year ([B3, Pessoas Físicas, fourth quarter 2024](https://b3.com.br/data/files/13/92/D5/D7/562179106B8BCB69AC094EA8/Book%20Pessoa%20Fisica%20-%204TRI%202024.pdf)), and 205,949 new investors in 2025, a rise of 4%, for almost 5.5 million and R$635 billion in custody ([B3, 16 January 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/noticias/bolsa-atrai-206-mil-novos-investidores-em-renda-variavel-em-2025-veja-estados-com-maior-representatividade.htm)). Its first-quarter 2026 book speaks of stability "na faixa de 5 milhões de investidores (CPFs distintos)", around 5 million distinct CPFs, and counts about 104 million Brazilians holding bank deposit products registered at B3 and 3.4 million in the Treasury's retail bond programme ([B3, Pessoas Físicas, first quarter 2026](https://b3.com.br/data/files/EA/80/8F/5D/FDFDD910E7DB5DD9AC094EA8/Book%20PF%201TRI%202026.pdf)).

Activity grew faster than headcount. B3 says the average number of trades a day is now 8.4 million, with a peak of 18 million in one day, against 10,000 to 15,000 a day in the open outcry era ([B3 Bora Investir, 30 September 2025](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/fim-do-pregao-viva-voz-faz-20-anos-e-b3-celebra-historia-com-exposicao/)).

## Who supervises what: the CVM, the Banco Central and BSM

The CVM was created on 7 December 1976 by Lei 6.385 to supervise, regulate, discipline and develop Brazil's securities market; it is an autarchy under a special regime linked to the Ministry of Finance, with independent administrative authority, fixed mandates for its directors and financial autonomy ([CVM, Sobre a CVM](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao-cvm/institucional/sobre-a-cvm)). In its original 1976 text, the law gave stock exchanges administrative, financial and patrimonial autonomy under the CVM's supervision and charged them, as auxiliary bodies of the CVM, with supervising their members and the operations on them (article 17) ([Senado Federal, Lei nº 6.385, de 7 de dezembro de 1976](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/548261/publicacao/15715128)).

The current market structure rules date from 10 June 2022, when the CVM issued Resolution 135, rewriting the rules on regulated markets and the entities that run them, and amended Resolution 35 to set brokers' best execution duty for a world in which several trading venues might list the same security ([CVM, 10 June 2022](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2022/cvm-moderniza-regras-sobre-mercados-organizados-e-melhor-execucao-de-ordens-de-clientes)). The CVM kept the existing self-regulation model instead of imposing a single self-regulator, so each market operator keeps its own Mecanismo de Ressarcimento de Prejuízos (MRP), its loss compensation mechanism ([CVM, 10 June 2022](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2022/cvm-moderniza-regras-sobre-mercados-organizados-e-melhor-execucao-de-ordens-de-clientes)). B3's self-regulatory arm is BSM ([B3, Princípios para Infraestruturas do Mercado Financeiro, March 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/55/F1/B4/04/04A11810C493CD08AC094EA8/B3%20PFMI%20DISCLOSURE%202022%20PT%20SEM%20MARCAS.pdf)), which inspects the certified software participants use ([B3, Manual de Procedimentos Operacionais de Negociação, 1 July 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/CF/D0/64/95/753D0A105BF9020AAC094EA8/MPO%20de%20Negociacao%20da%20B3.pdf)) and must include DMA operations in its work programme ([CVM, Resolução CVM 35, 26 May 2021](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/cvm_institucional/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol035.pdf)).

The Banco Central do Brasil (BCB) watches the plumbing behind the trades. B3's disclosure says its two clearinghouses, the Câmara B3 for derivatives, equities, private fixed income and securities lending and the Câmara de Câmbio B3 for the interbank foreign exchange market, are considered systemically important by the BCB, and that the Conselho Monetário Nacional (CMN), the BCB and the CVM are the main bodies regulating Brazil's financial and capital markets, each in its own sphere ([B3, Princípios para Infraestruturas do Mercado Financeiro, March 2022](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/55/F1/B4/04/04A11810C493CD08AC094EA8/B3%20PFMI%20DISCLOSURE%202022%20PT%20SEM%20MARCAS.pdf)). The rules that reshaped market structure in Brazil and abroad are compared in [The rules that rewired markets](/learn/the-rules-that-rewired-markets-reg-ats-reg-nms-mifid-and-brazil); surveillance and regulatory reporting by the CVM belong to a later part.

## What to remember

- B3 descends from the Bolsa Livre (1890), Bovespa (named 1967), BM&F (1986), BM&FBovespa (2008) and the merger with Cetip (March 2017).
- Bovespa's floor closed on 30 September 2005 and BM&F's on 30 June 2009, by B3's reference form and its museum; a 2025 B3 news page says mid-2006.
- PUMA was developed with CME Group under the 2010 partnership and entered operation in the BM&F segment (2011), the Bovespa segment (2013) and private fixed income (2014); orders enter through EntryPoint, based on FIX 4.4, with a binary SBE version since 2023 and 2024.
- Only authorized participants trade; a client may send its own proprietary orders through B3's sponsored direct connection, under a broker's responsibility and limits, and the CVM requires origin identification and pre-trade risk controls for DMA.
- Co-location sits in Santana de Parnaíba, with equal-length cables and quoted latencies of 22 microseconds to the order entry gateways.
- Retail investors in variable income rose from about 3.4 million distinct CPFs in October 2021 to almost 5.5 million at the end of 2025, by B3's count; accounts and CPFs are different measures.
